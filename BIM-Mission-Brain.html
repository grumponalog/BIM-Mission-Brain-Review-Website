📖 Founding story & leadership

Opened as Little Buckeye Children's Museum in April 2011 on West Fourth Street; incorporated as an Ohio 501(c)(3) in 2010. Purchased a 30,000 sq ft building at 175 West Third Street in late 2018 (joint capital campaign with the Renaissance Performing Arts Association — the Imagination District), renovated through COVID, rebranded to Buckeye Imagination Museum on May 23, 2022, and opened the new home August 10, 2022. Phase 3 built out the second-floor gallery (now The Zone).

BIM works to make a visit easy for every family. ✅ 501(c)(3)-compliant sponsor phrasing

Approved verbs: Sponsored by · Presented by · Proudly supported by · Made possible by · Generously supported by.

Example: "Electrical Exhibit made possible by FirstEnergy." · "Train Tables proudly supported by Mr. Bill's Wood Fired Pizza."

Never use (triggers taxable advertising — IRC §513(i))Prices, discounts, promo codes · comparative/superlative language ("best," "#1," "award-winning") · calls to action ("buy now," "switch and save") · endorsements · sponsor having final wording approval.

🚫 Banned & deprecated language

  • Hard ban (501c3): comparatives/CTAs/discounts in any sponsor recognition.
  • Brand anti-patterns: "babyish" tone (Justin's hardest no), over-effusive language, corporate jargon to families, apologetic openings, "unlike other museums."
  • Retire: "Mansfield/Richland Discovery School" (never was BIM's name) · pre-unification mission · "Class A–E" tier names · "children's museum" alone as primary descriptor.
"Social suicide" insight — internal use only, NEVER externalJustin: a 13-year-old won't have a birthday party "at Buckeye Imagination Museum" but will "at The Zone." This is the strategic justification for The Zone endorsed brand — explain why The Zone exists, never say it externally.

🤖 AI usage — the maker-checker rule

AI Teammates operate on the IF System Standard's 8 stages. The pair that never collapses: Stage 5 (AI Review — AI self-checks) → Stage 6 (Human Review — named Reviewer approves before ship).

AI Teammates MAY

  • Draft external comms, research the brain, summarize, assemble packets, suggest, coach.

AI Teammates may NOT

  • Send anything without Stage 6 · make financial/acceptance/hiring decisions · commit BIM to contracts · use a child's image/story without documented consent · cross into 501(c)(3) advertising · operate outside the Mission Brain.
Drafts onlyAI Teammates never send external communications directly — they draft for human review. Every run is logged (input, draft, reviewer, decision, output, timestamp) for the Quarterly Health Check.

📣 AI disclosure & sensitive content

Disclose AI involvement

  • Yes: donor letters (footer), grant narratives, AI-generated annual-report sections, AI imagery of people.
  • No (industry standard): marketing copy, sponsor announcements, press releases, internal board packets.

Auto-escalate (no publish without review)

  • Anything involving a named minor; disability/mental-health/medical info; race/religion/orientation/politics; sponsor/donor in negative context; non-public financials; personnel info; legal/IRS matters; AI imagery of people; unclear/expired consent.
